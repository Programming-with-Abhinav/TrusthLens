TrustLens AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Version: 1.3.0

Zero-Tracking Core Commitment: TrustLens AI does not track, record, or sell your web browsing history. We believe security and truth verification should never require sacrificing personal privacy.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Selected Text for Verification: When you select 4 or more words on a webpage, that snippet is sent to the TrustLens verification gateway to retrieve fact-checking sources. We do not link this text to your identity or store it permanently.
  • Active Tab URL: When you open the TrustLens popup to scan a page, the current tab URL is inspected for phishing patterns, crypto-drainers, and known threats. We log only aggregate anonymized safety levels (e.g. "Safe", "Dangerous").
  • User-Requested Summaries: When you click “Summarize page,” visible article text (or selected text) and its URL are sent to your configured TrustLens backend. Raw page text is not saved; only a hash-keyed summary cache entry and a generic summary activity record are stored.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you create an account to view your dashboard, we store your email address and aggregate daily counters (e.g., 5 claims checked today). We never store which claims you checked.

2. Permissions Justification

  • activeTab: Required strictly to read the URL of the tab when the user clicks the extension icon to perform a security scan.
  • storage: Used strictly to save your preferred backend URL and your optional Supabase authentication token across browser sessions.
  • clipboardWrite: Used only after you click “Copy summary” to copy the generated summary.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide factual verdicts and threat intelligence, TrustLens queries:

  • Google Fact Check Tools API (retrieves public ClaimReview records)
  • Google Web Risk API (identifies known malware and social engineering links)
  • Supabase (manages user authentication and daily quota counting)

4. Data Deletion and Control

You can delete your account and all associated usage records at any time directly through the TrustLens Web Dashboard. Account deletion immediately cascades across all databases.

5. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or privacy concerns, please open an issue on the TrustLens GitHub repository or visit trustlens.ai.
